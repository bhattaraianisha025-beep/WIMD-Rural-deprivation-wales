## Data governance safeguard

This project uses small-area (LSOA-level) official statistics only.
No individual-level or personally identifiable data is included. Where
outputs identify specific small areas (for example, lists of
neighbourhoods below a deprivation threshold), these are drawn directly
from published WIMD rankings, which are themselves public at LSOA
level. No additional disclosure risk is introduced beyond what is
already public in the source data; area names are not linked to any
individual-level information.
